# Prompt: Quiz Me

Use this when you want to check whether you've actually reached "understand"
tier on your current track before moving to "build."

```
Read progress/STATE.md and curriculum/<current-track>.md. Quiz me on the
tier 1 concepts for my current track — one question at a time, no multiple
choice, make me explain it in my own words. Push back if my answer is
technically right but shows a shaky mental model. Tell me honestly at the
end whether I've earned tier 1, and update progress/STATE.md accordingly.
```
